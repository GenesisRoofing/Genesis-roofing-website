A clear starting point

Repair or replace?

A leak does not always mean you need a new roof. Age, repeated trouble spots, widespread damage, and the condition of the existing materials all matter. We look at the roof and explain the options before recommending a scope of work.

If replacement makes sense, your estimate can describe the proposed materials, removal and disposal, roof preparation, flashing and ventilation details, and cleanup. The exact scope depends on your home.

What to expect

From assessment to finished roof

1. Review the roof

Tell us about leaks, storm damage, or concerns. We assess the visible condition and discuss whether repair or replacement is appropriate.

2. Choose a roofing system

We discuss shingle or metal options that suit the roof, appearance you want, and project budget. Explore metal roofing.

3. Confirm the written scope

Review what the estimate includes, such as tear-off, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, decking allowances, cleanup, and any product-specific warranty terms.

4. Install and inspect

Once the scope and schedule are agreed, we complete the work and review the finished roof and site with you.
