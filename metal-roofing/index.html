What we discuss during a metal roof estimate

Existing roof and structure

We look at the roof's condition and discuss what preparation may be needed before installation. The approach depends on the home and the proposed roofing system.

Appearance and details

Color, panel style, trim, and flashing details all affect the finished appearance. We'll discuss options that fit your home and budget.

Scope and timing

An estimate should make the proposed work and next steps clear. Tell us whether you're replacing an existing roof or planning a new installation.

Maintenance questions

Ask about the care requirements and warranty terms for the particular materials being considered. These vary by product and installation.
